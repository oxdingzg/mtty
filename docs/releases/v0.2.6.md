# mtty v0.2.6

[简体中文](v0.2.6.zh-CN.md)

## Crash fix: runaway reflow in a one-column pane

A terminal pane that is briefly collapsed to a single column could exhaust memory
and abort the whole process. The grid engine (alacritty 0.25) needs at least two
columns to reflow a double-width character; at one column the wide cell was
re-inserted into the remaining content on every iteration, appending rows without
bound, and the scrollback cap only applied after the loop returned. A pane that
held wide text (CJK, emoji) and then resized to one column — during a window
resize, or while a split was momentarily collapsed — pinned the UI thread (frames
stopped) while private memory climbed into the gigabytes until the allocation
failure aborted.

Terminal grids are now clamped to a minimum of two columns at construction,
resize, PTY sizing and pane layout, and the PTY dimensions the shell sees stay
consistent with the screen. (#150, #151)

## Verification and packages

CJK and emoji content is reflowed through narrow and wide sizes with its contents
preserved, and zero-size construction and query/PTY sizes are covered. Full
cross-platform CI — including the Windows test job and the rendering and Release
performance gates — passed before the fix merged.

Packages: macOS ARM64 and Intel ZIP bundles; Linux x86-64 tar.gz, AppImage and deb;
Windows x86-64 ZIP and MSI. Publication verifies package contents, update manifest
checksums and detached signatures.

The scrollable-tab and titlebar fixes from v0.2.5 remain included.
